Mace and spear techniques

The 27 mace, spear, wind charge and elytra moves a bot can know, each one its own switch.

Difficulty is how well a bot plays; techniques are which moves it knows at all. Each technique is an ordinary setting, so it can be on for one bot or faction and off for the rest. The moves bots already used start on; the newer ones start off.

/pvpbot techniques                                    every technique, on or off
/pvpbot techniques bot <bot>                          one bot's
/pvpbot techniques faction <faction>                  one faction's
/pvpbot techniques [bot <bot>|faction <f>] <technique> <on|off|variant>

They're also on a page of the menu.

Notes on a few#

  • Spear-macers actually smash: the spear dash solves the velocity that lands on the target, and the smash is always swung with the mace.
  • Wind-charge cancels are rare: 10% of an opponent's launches (wc-cancel-chance), never twice within 15 seconds.
  • Chained elytra mace (elytra-chain, on by default): when a Wind Burst smash throws a bot up, it opens its elytra at the top, fires one rocket back over you and drops again. It keeps going until it misses, runs out of rockets or lands four hits, and each hit after the first is harder to land.
  • Rocket mace picks how it climbs: climb (the usual), spiral (round you), angled (out, then a long run in) or overhead (straight up over you).
  • Pearl catch for the pearl launch: smart picks per situation and is the one to use.
