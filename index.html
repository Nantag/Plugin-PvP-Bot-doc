PvpBot

AI-driven PvP bots for Paper and Spigot servers. They are real fake players: they fight, move, gear up and think like people, not like mobs.

Start in 5 minutes → All commands Settings

What PvpBot is#

PvpBot adds bots that are real players (with no client attached). To the server and to other plugins they are people like anyone else: they have an inventory, armour, hunger, potions and effects. They fight with the same weapons and tricks as a real player: jump crits, the mace, shields, bows, pearls, cobwebs, water buckets.

The goal is an opponent to train against and record with, not a terminator. Nothing a bot does is frame-perfect: the skill dial (0–100) sets how fast its reactions, timing and aim are, and even at 100 there is a gap to play around.

What it can do#

⚔️CombatMelee, mace, elytra mace, cart PvP, bows with lead prediction, shields. 🧭MovementA* pathfinding, bhop, bridging and pillaring, boats, guards, patrols. 🏳️FactionsWar, peace, alliances, leaders, overfactions and per-faction settings. 🎒Kits and presetsSaved loadouts and difficulty profiles in one command. 📜ScriptsWhole scenarios saved under a name, with parameters and pauses. 🐺CompanionsBots handed to a player, who orders them around in chat. 🎭Skins and namesReal or random skins, themed name tables. 👻GhostsScenic NPCs that replay a recorded path or stare at you.

At a glance#

  • Servers: Paper 1.21.11 and 26.2, Spigot 1.21.11 and 26.2 (separate jars).
  • Commands: /pvpbot (alias /bot) and /ghost.
  • Permission: pvpbot.admin, ops by default.
  • Dependencies: none required. SkinsRestorer is supported if installed.
