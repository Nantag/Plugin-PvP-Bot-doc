Arena, stats and debug view

Test a setting with repeatable bot duels, keep score of every fight with a bot, and see what a bot is thinking.

Arena#

/pvpbot arena spawns two bots where you stand and has them fight round after round. It then reports per side: wins, time to kill, hits and damage per hit, aimed swings that connected, crits, smashes, best combo, totems popped, heals, and mid-fight inventory opens. Change a setting, run it again, compare.

/pvpbot arena start <kitA> <kitB> [rounds] [skillA] [skillB]
/pvpbot arena stop
/pvpbot arena report

Debug view#

/pvpbot debugview floats a line over a bot showing which part of its AI is in charge, its target and how the trade looks, what it has read about you, its aim, and its latest call ("whiffed", "their shield is down: all out").

/pvpbot debugview <bot|all>

Stats#

PvpBot keeps score of every fight with a bot on at least one side: kills, deaths, hits, damage and melee accuracy (swings against hits landed), for every bot and every player who fights one. It's a scoreboard for the bots, not for the whole server's PvP. The numbers are kept across restarts in stats.yml.

/pvpbot stats [name]                              yours, or anyone's
/pvpbot stats top [kills|kd|accuracy|damage]      the top ten
/pvpbot stats reset <name|all>                    needs pvpbot.admin

Seeing stats needs only pvpbot.stats, which every player has by default; resetting them needs pvpbot.admin.
