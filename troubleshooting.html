Troubleshooting

The usual reasons a bot won't move, won't take damage or won't attack, and how to fix them.

Always start here /pvpbot diagnose <bot> checks the bot and tells you what's blocking it: game mode, damage immunity, login plugins, plugins cancelling damage, movement or knockback, and the last knockback it took.

The bot hops on the spot and won't move#

It's almost always a login plugin (AuthMe, LoginTo…). A bot can't type /login, so the plugin keeps it locked.

  1. Check that auth-autologin is true (it is by default): it registers bots as already logged in.
  2. If your login plugin isn't one PvpBot recognises, /pvpbot diagnose shows it. In that case set the login plugin to ignore players with the bots' names, or disable it to test.

The bot takes no damage#

  • Invulnerable flag: some login plugins set it and never clear it. With force-vulnerable: true (the default) PvpBot clears it. diagnose tells you if this is the cause.
  • Region protection or PvP off: WorldGuard, safe zones and the like cancel damage. It's better to move the fight out of the protected area. As a last resort there's /pvpbot set bypass-damage-protection true, but it overrides all protection, legitimate protection included.
  • Game mode: a bot in creative or spectator takes no damage.

The bot won't attack me#

  • It was just spawned: it waits spawn-grace seconds, then sizes you up for reaction-ticks.
  • It can't see you: with detection on it needs line of sight. Crouching or invisible, you're noticed from closer.
  • You're in creative or spectator (attack-invincible is off).
  • A preset made it passive (/pvpbot preset shows which is active), or auto-target or target-players are off, perhaps just for its faction (/pvpbot faction info).
  • You're allies or at peace: see Factions.
  • It's paused, hiding or retreating.

The bot fights with its fists#

It has no weapons. Give it a kit or /pvpbot givebow <bot>. auto-equip must be on for it to hold the best weapon by itself.

Lag with lots of bots#

  • Raise target-scan-interval (say from 10 to 20): looking for targets is the most expensive thing a bot does. When its target dies or leaves, a bot still looks again at once.
  • Lower max-target-distance.
  • Turn wander off for bots that don't need to move, or pause them.

Odd stats or speed after a boss or an item#

Another plugin added an attribute modifier (health, speed, damage) and never removed it. /pvpbot resetattr <player|bot> removes every modifier and restores a player's correct base values. It works on real players too.

The world folder fills up with bot files#

Bots are real players, so the server saves data, stats and advancements for them like anyone else. /pvpbot clearplayerdata deletes those files for bots that no longer exist. It leaves active bots, kits, factions, scripts and config alone.

The plugin loads but nothing works#

  • Check you installed the right jar for your platform and version (see Installation).
  • Read the console at startup. A kits.yml copied from another server may fail to load: PvpBot still starts, without kits, and says so in the console.

Companions aren't put away#

Check companions.holding-world: it must be the name of a world that exists on your server. See Companions.
