--- title: Menus section: Features order: 45 lead: The in-game menus: every setting, every bot, kits and factions, without typing commands. ---

Every button runs the matching /pvpbot command, so permissions and replies are the same as typing it. The menus work on the Spigot edition too.

The hub

/pvpbot menu
  • Bots: a head for each bot. Its panel opens its bag or ender chest live, gives it a kit, restocks it, sets its faction, sends it to follow or fight, pauses or removes it.
  • Kits: edit, wear, hand out, delete, or make a new one (named in chat).
  • Factions: members, kit odds, recruit bots nearby, follow, formation, war, peace and alliance, uniform, disband.
  • Presets, settings and techniques.

The settings menu

/pvpbot settings opens a paged menu of every setting. It's built from the same list the commands check against, so nothing is ever missing from it.

  • Left-click toggles or steps a value up, right-click steps it down, and shift steps by ten. Q takes a faction's or bot's own value off.
  • Filter by group, search by word, and nudge the whole difficulty a preset up or down from the bottom row.
/pvpbot settings                                 the menu
/pvpbot settings search <word>                   the menu, only what matches
/pvpbot settings bot <bot> | faction <faction>   one bot's or one faction's values
/pvpbot settings list [group|word]               printed in chat instead
/pvpbot faction settings <faction> [word]        the faction's screen, with what it changes marked

The kit editor

The kit editor lays a kit out like an inventory (bag, hotbar, armour, off-hand), with a second page for the kit's ender chest. A new name makes a new, empty kit.

/pvpbot kit edit <name>
/pvpbot kit rename <old> <new>      faction kit tables follow the new name
