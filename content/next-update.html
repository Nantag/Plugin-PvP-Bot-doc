Just released

The last release brought, among other things:

  • jars for Minecraft 26.3, Paper and Spigot;
  • difficulty knobs, presets and playstyles, and bots that play like people;
  • aim that decides hits, senses and smarter fighting;
  • 27 mace and spear techniques;
  • regearing from an ender chest or a shulker box;
  • menus for every setting, bot, kit and faction, and a kit editor;
  • formations, marches, uniforms and armies;
  • Orbital Strike Cannon V8 and commands on patrol waypoints;
  • the arena, stats and debug view, and /pvpbot diagnose <player>;
  • the add-on API for other plugins.

/pvpbot tp rift left PvpBot in that release; tp portal and tp pearl stay.
