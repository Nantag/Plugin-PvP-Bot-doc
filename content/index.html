PvpBot

Bots that join your server as real players and fight the way people do: jump crits, shields, the mace, bows, pearls, cobwebs and water buckets. Built to train against and record with, not to win every fight.

Download on Modrinth Quick start

What it is

A PvpBot bot is a player with no client attached. To the server and every other plugin it is a person like anyone else: it has an inventory, armour, hunger, potion effects and a skin, and it takes knockback and damage the vanilla way.

Nothing it does is frame-perfect. One skill dial, from 0 to 100, sets how quickly it reacts, how well it times a swing and how far off its aim lands, and it splits into knobs when you want finer control. Even at 100 there is a gap to play around. Bots read you, space, bait and tilt like people, know 27 mace and spear techniques, regear from an ender chest, march in formations and fight in armies that hold or break. And there's a menu for every setting.
