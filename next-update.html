/pvpbot diagnose works on players#

/pvpbot diagnose <bot> explains why a bot can't fight. The new form answers the opposite question: why bots can't hurt a particular player.

/pvpbot diagnose <player>
/pvpbot diagnose <player> reset

It reads the player's state the way the game's damage checks see it: game mode, the invulnerable flag, the game's own "client still loading" protection, hit invulnerability, a raised shield, a shared scoreboard team, PvP in the world, and the client brand and version (through ViaVersion if installed).

Then it lists every bot within 48 blocks, with whether that bot would fight the player, what it is targeting, and whether it can see them.

From the moment the server starts, every bot hit aimed at a real player is logged. The report shows what became of each one:

OutcomeWhat it means
Turned awayThe game refused the hit before any plugin saw it. The reason is checked on the spot: hit invulnerability, creative or spectator, an invulnerable flag, the loading protection, a team without friendly fire, or PvP off.
CancelledA plugin stopped it. The report says at which event priority, which narrows down the plugin.
ShieldTheir raised shield took it.
ZeroedIt went through with no damage left (a plugin, or armour and resistance).
LandedIt hurt them.

It ends with a one-line verdict. reset starts a fresh log. Melee swings, mace smashes, spear jabs and elytra strikes are all logged, and so are bot arrows. Both the Paper and the Spigot editions have it.

Blinded bots can't see#

A bot with Blindness or Darkness no longer spots targets by sight. It only notices someone who comes within touching distance (about a block and a half). It still remembers who it was already fighting. A splash of blindness is now a real way to break off a fight.

Weapon add-ons#

For plugin developers. PvpBot now has an extension point, BotArsenal, that lets another plugin teach bots to use its own gear: guns, grenades, medical items, special armour. With no add-on installed nothing changes.

A handler registers once and is asked at fixed points in every bot's combat tick:

HookWhen
overrideRight after the target is chosen, ahead of eating, pearls and retreats.
fightWith a target, ahead of every built-in attack. Handing the tick back falls through to PvpBot's own melee and ranged play.
idleWith no target (or while hiding), ahead of guarding and escorting.
blocksSightWhenever a bot looks for a target: something only the add-on knows about (a smoke cloud) can hide a player.

Three more keep PvpBot's own gear logic out of the way: holdsHand stops the weapon auto-equip while the add-on holds an item on purpose, reserved keeps the bow and crossbow code off the add-on's items, and armourScore lets the armour picker rank the add-on's armour.

The first add-on is FirearmsBots: its bots fight with guns, grenades, cover, plates, meds and revives.

BotArsenal.register(new BotArsenal.Handler() {
    @Override
    public boolean fight(Player bot, Entity target, BotSettings settings) {
        // fight with your gear; return false to let PvpBot fight as usual
        return false;
    }
});
